---
name: playbill-blind-spots
description: Identify unresolved decisions that change a design and formulate questions in dependency order.
---

Compare the brief with repository evidence and recorded decisions. Keep questions
whose alternatives change interfaces, architecture, authorized scope, or acceptance
criteria. Resolve discoverable facts by reading; choose routine reversible details
using project conventions and record the assumption when it matters.

For each material decision, identify prerequisites, viable choices, a recommended
choice, and the consequence of guessing wrong. Return the currently answerable
questions in consequence order, plus decisions still dependent on another answer.
Incorporate supplied answers and prior approvals; a settled decision stays settled
unless new evidence invalidates it. Explicit delegation to choose is itself an answer.

The result distinguishes settled decisions, routine assumptions, and unresolved
material decisions. An empty unresolved set is a useful result.
